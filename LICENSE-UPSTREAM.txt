BiliDing (B钉) is an independent userscript. Its three-pane layout vocabulary
(.im-rail / .im-list-panel / .im-chat-panel), the skin token palette and the
general "wrap a content site into an IM shell" approach are inspired by
czm15053/linuxdo-idea-ui (xhs module, dingtalk skin) and its Bilibili port
Jack-Cherish/BiliShu. No code is copy-pasted; the implementation, data layer
and DingTalk visual polish are original to this project.
